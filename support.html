Tovia Support

Tovia is a private space for the two of you. If something isn't working, or you have a question, email info@fndigistudio.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days.

Pairing with your partner

One of you starts your space and sends the one-time invite link. The other opens the link, or pastes it under "Have an invite?" on the pairing screen. Invites expire, so if one has, send a new one.

Tovia Premium

One subscription covers you both. To cancel, change or check your subscription, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. If Premium doesn't show after buying, open Settings in Tovia, tap Tovia Premium, then Restore purchases.

Your data

You can download a copy of your data, or delete your account, from Settings in Tovia. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel that in your Apple ID settings. More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
