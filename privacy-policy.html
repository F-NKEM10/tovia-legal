Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Tovia is a private app for two people in a relationship. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it is kept, who can see it, and the choices you have. We have tried to write it plainly.

Who we are. Tovia is published by Folefac Nkem, 182-184 High Street North, East Ham, London E6 2JA, United Kingdom ("we", "us"). We are the controller of your personal data under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). You can contact us about anything in this policy at info@fndigistudio.com.

The short version

  • In Tovia, only you and your partner can see what you share. There is no public profile, feed, search or chat with strangers.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools. We count how Tovia is used in a private, pseudonymous way so we can improve it (see "Usage information" below).
  • Your data is encrypted on the way to our servers and while it is stored, in London, United Kingdom.
  • You can export a copy of your data, and delete your account, at any time from Settings.

What we collect

Your account. When you sign in with Apple we receive a user identifier and an email address (often a private relay address that Apple creates, which hides your real email). You choose a display name, and you can add a profile photo.

Your shared space. Everything you add so that you and your partner can see it, including: daily question answers and daily sentences; Tovia Now photos and captions; notes, checklists and their photos; shared lists; calendar events and special dates; trips, places, photos and reflections; bucket list items and memories; Our Story milestones; time capsules; jar notes; home and dream ideas; song picks; reactions; Monthly Check-in answers; Love Language quiz results; extra daily questions and answers; Date Match choices; and your games, answers and scores.

Private notes. Remember Them entries are visible only to the person who wrote them, not to their partner. Some things stay sealed from your partner until the moment you both agreed, such as a time capsule until it opens and an answer until you have both replied; until then we hold them, and we do not read them.

Location (optional). Only if you switch on "how far apart are we". We take a single reading, round it to roughly the nearest 11 km before it is saved, and show it only to your partner. You can switch this off at any time, and the saved location is removed.

Notifications (optional). If you allow notifications we store your device's push token and your notification settings, so your partner's actions and the reminders you chose can reach you. Notification text never includes what you or your partner wrote.

18+ content settings. If you both choose to see mature card content, we record that each of you confirmed you are an adult and opted in.

Premium. If you subscribe, Apple handles the payment. We receive a transaction record from Apple (an ID, the product, its status and dates) so we can unlock Premium for you and your partner. We never see your card or bank details. If you start a free trial we set a reminder on your own phone for shortly before it ends; it is not sent through us. If you tap "Ask your partner" about Premium, we send them a short notification with your display name.

Usage information. So we can tell whether Tovia is working and what to improve, we record a small set of events: that the app was opened on a day, which main areas were used (at most once a day each), when a game or card deck starts and ends, when a date is confirmed, when someone meets a Premium limit or views Premium, when you export your data, your app and iOS version, and whether your space has Premium. These are recorded against a pseudonymous code made from your account with a secret key, not your name or email, and never include anything you or your partner wrote or shared. Once your account is deleted the code can no longer be linked to you. These events are kept for 25 months and are only ever looked at in totals. We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking service for this.

Our support access. A very small number of people on our team can use a private admin console to see totals (for example how many spaces are active), to find an account by its email or ID to help with a support request, and to switch a space's Premium on or off. It cannot show what you or your partner wrote or shared. It needs a password and a code from an authenticator app, and every change made in it is recorded in a log that cannot be edited.

Things we do not collect. Contacts, your photo library (you pick photos one at a time through Apple's picker), health data, browsing history, advertising identifiers, or what you tap and scroll through on each screen.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To provide Tovia to you and your partner (performance of our contract with you): storing and syncing your shared space, running games, sending the notifications you chose, and providing Premium.
  • With your consent: location, notifications, 18+ content. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings.
  • To keep Tovia safe and working and to improve it (our legitimate interests): preventing abuse such as one subscription being used for many couples, fixing problems, protecting accounts, and understanding in totals which features are used (usage information). You can object to this at any time by emailing us.
  • To meet legal obligations, for example keeping records Apple or the law requires.

Who your data is shared with

We share data only with the services that run Tovia, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in services), hosted on Amazon Web Services in London (eu-west-2).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications (APNs), the App Store for subscriptions, Apple Maps for maps, and the Apple music catalogue when you search for a song (your search words are sent to Apple to find the song).
  • Wikimedia: photos in the Where In The World game load from Wikimedia's servers, which see the request like any website does.

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK (for example Apple in the United States). Where that happens it is protected by the safeguards UK law requires, such as adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Your shared space is kept for as long as your account and your space exist. If you delete something, it is removed.
  • Tovia Now: on Free, you can see the last 3 days of past photos and they are kept for 7 days; on Premium, you can see them all and they are kept while Premium is active.
  • If Premium ends, the extras it unlocked (for example extra photos) are locked rather than deleted, and kept for 30 days in case you resubscribe. After that window they may be deleted. You can export a copy first.
  • If you leave a shared space or delete your account, your personal account data is removed. Content in the shared space belongs to both of you, so your partner keeps the space; you can export a copy first.
  • Spaces that are no longer used may be deleted after a period of inactivity. We will tell you in the app before anything is removed.
  • Backups are kept for a short time by our hosting provider and then overwritten.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent. Much of this you can do yourself in Settings (download a copy, and delete). The copy includes what you and your partner have written and shared and your photos, up to a size limit that the file says it reached; anything still sealed from you stays sealed. For anything else, email info@fndigistudio.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Security

Your data is encrypted on its way to and from our servers (TLS 1.2 or higher) and while it is stored, including your photos and our backups (AES-256). Access rules let only the two members of a space read it through Tovia, and photos are kept in private storage, reachable only through short-lived signed links. Photos saved on your phone are protected by your device's own encryption, and you can lock Tovia with Face ID.

Tovia is not end-to-end encrypted. Your data has to be readable on our servers for Tovia to work, so the small number of people who run the service could technically reach it. We do not look at what you write or share, our support tools do not show it, and we would only access it if the law required us to or to fix a fault you have asked us to look at.

Age

Tovia is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 is using Tovia, please tell us at info@fndigistudio.com.

Changes to this policy

If we make a significant change we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.
